# Nyx R7 Setup Guide

## First use
The R7 battery ships at about 40 percent charge. Charge it fully with the included 65 W USB-C charger before your first scan. A full charge from empty takes 2 hours.

## Powering on
Press and hold the power button for 3 seconds. The R7 runs an automatic calibration on every power-up, which takes about 90 seconds. Keep the scanner still and on a flat, stable surface while it calibrates.

## Pairing with Nyx Studio
Install Nyx Studio on your computer or phone, then enable Wi-Fi on the R7 from the touchscreen. Select your R7 from the device list in Nyx Studio and enter the 6-digit code shown on the scanner. Pairing is required for firmware updates and for exporting point clouds.

## Manual calibration
If scans drift or accuracy drops, run a manual calibration from Settings > Calibration. Place the R7 on a flat surface and start the routine; it takes about 90 seconds. Manual calibration is recommended after large temperature changes.

## Transferring scans
For small scans, transfer over Wi-Fi 6 to Nyx Studio. For large datasets, use a USB-C cable, which is significantly faster than Wi-Fi. The R7 has no SD card slot, so all scans live on the 256 GB internal SSD until transferred.
