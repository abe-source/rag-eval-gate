# Nyx R7 Technical Specifications

## Ranging and accuracy
The Nyx R7 has a measurement range of 0.3 m to 120 m. Range accuracy is plus or minus 3 mm at 50 m. The scanner captures up to 640,000 points per second. The field of view is 360 degrees horizontal by 285 degrees vertical.

## Laser
The R7 uses a Class 1 (eye-safe) laser operating at a 905 nm wavelength. No protective eyewear is required during operation.

## Power
The R7 is powered by a swappable 5,200 mAh lithium-ion battery, rated for approximately 4.5 hours of continuous scanning. A full charge takes 2 hours using the included 65 W USB-C Power Delivery charger. The battery ships at roughly 40 percent charge and must be fully charged before first use.

## Storage and connectivity
The R7 has 256 GB of internal SSD storage. There is no removable SD card slot. Data transfers over Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.2, or USB-C.

## Export formats
Scans export to the R7's native .rvz format, or to the .rvx interchange format for third-party tools.

## Physical
The R7 weighs 1.15 kg with the battery installed and measures 180 x 95 x 62 mm. It carries an IP54 rating (dust-protected and splash-resistant) and operates in temperatures from -10 C to 45 C. The current firmware version is 3.2.1.
