# Nyx R7 Troubleshooting

## The scanner will not power on
The battery is most likely fully depleted. Connect the 65 W USB-C charger and wait at least 30 minutes before trying again. If it still does not power on after charging, contact Corvid Instruments support.

## Scans drift or accuracy is poor
Run a manual calibration from Settings > Calibration on a flat surface. Confirm you are operating within the rated temperature range of -10 C to 45 C, as accuracy degrades outside it.

## Overheating warning
If the internal temperature exceeds 60 C, the R7 pauses scanning and shows an overheat warning. Move to a cooler location and let the device rest before resuming.

## Firmware update fails
Firmware is updated only through Nyx Studio. The R7 must have more than 20 percent battery before an update will start. Keep the scanner connected and do not power it off during the update.

## Error codes
- E01: sensor obstruction. Clean the scanner window and clear anything blocking the field of view.
- E02: IMU calibration failed. Restart the R7 on a flat, stable surface.
- E03: storage full. Transfer and delete scans to free space on the 256 GB SSD.
- E04: over-temperature. Same as the overheating warning above.
