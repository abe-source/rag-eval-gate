# Nyx R7 Portable LiDAR Scanner

The Nyx R7 is a handheld LiDAR scanner made by Corvid Instruments, first released in 2025. It is designed for surveyors, architects, and reality-capture professionals who need survey-grade point clouds without a tripod-mounted terrestrial scanner. The R7 is fully self-contained: it captures, processes, and stores scans on the device, with no laptop required in the field.

A single operator can walk through a site and capture a complete 3D point cloud in a continuous scan. The R7 uses simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) to stitch the moving scan together in real time, so there is no separate registration step for most jobs.

Scans are reviewed on the built-in 2.4-inch touchscreen or streamed live to the Nyx Studio companion app over Wi-Fi. Nyx Studio runs on Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android, and is the only supported way to update firmware and export processed point clouds.

## Kit and pricing

The R7 base kit sells for 8,900 EUR and ships with the scanner, one 5,200 mAh battery, a USB-C charger, and a hard case.

## Warranty

Corvid Instruments covers the R7 with a 2-year limited warranty.

## Accessories

A second battery and the Field Charging Dock are sold separately.
